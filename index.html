    SGPA / CGPA Formula

    SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)
    CGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)

    Percentage Conversion

    Percentage = CGPA × 10
